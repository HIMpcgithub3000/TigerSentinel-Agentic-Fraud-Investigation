## Post for LinkedIn

🚀 Thrilled to present **CaseGuard**—our submission for the **TigerGraph Agentic Fraud Investigation Hackathon (Hacker House Goa 2026)**!

Traditional fraud systems treat machine learning risk scores as ground truth and rely on black-box classification. In financial crimes, that leads to high false positives, missed syndicate rings, and unexplainable alerts.

With **CaseGuard**, we built a truly autonomous, uncertainty-gated **Agentic Fraud Investigator** powered by **@TigerGraphDB** and **LangGraph**:

🔍 **Multi-Hop Graph Intelligence:** Parameterized GSQL queries (`customer_baseline`, `card_window`, `device_ring`) with strict `as_of_timestamp` boundaries to prevent temporal data leakage.
📋 **Structured Evidence Ledger:** Every observation becomes an immutable, cited evidence item (`EV-001..EV-NNN`) tracking supporting and contradicting hypotheses.
⚖️ **Competing Hypotheses & Sufficiency Gating:** Distinguishes genuine anomalies from legitimate baseline travel, resolving false alarms before destructive blocks occur.
🛡️ **Dual-Phase Governance & SAR Drafting:** Explicitly records Initial vs Final Next-Best Actions across a 4-tier approval matrix (`auto`, `L1`, `L2`, `compliance`) and drafts grounded FinCEN SAR narratives.
🧠 **Long-Term Case Memory:** Atomically writes case records back into TigerGraph's graph memory to inform future investigations.
📊 **100% Benchmark Pass Rate:** Evaluated on all 20 IEEE-CIS benchmark cases with 100% submission contract compliance.

Huge thanks to @TigerGraphDB and the Hacker House Goa mentors for organizing this cutting-edge hackathon!
